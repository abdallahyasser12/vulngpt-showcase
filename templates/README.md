# Reusable assessment templates

These blank templates support planning and documenting authorized application-security assessments.

- [Assessment Plan](assessment-plan.md) — target scope, identities, operational constraints, data handling, success criteria, and approvals.
- [Finding Report](finding-report.md) — concise, evidence-led reporting with context, impact, limitations, remediation, and retest fields.

Templates are starting points only. They do not grant authorization, define a contractual engagement, or replace the system owner's approval and handling requirements. Adapt them to the relevant organization's policies before use.
