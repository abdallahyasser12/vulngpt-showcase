# Gauntlet G1 — Publication Status

<small>Publication status: **Under review — not approved for marketing use**</small>

## Why this page no longer publishes a score

A previous version of this document published internal G1 benchmark figures alongside an explanation of the adjudicated denominator and row-level categories. The explanation did not reconcile arithmetically across the stated headline, category counts, and adjudication narrative.

Because the public explanation is internally inconsistent, the figures are withdrawn from approved public marketing use until the underlying frozen evidence and scoring explanation have been independently reconciled by the project team.

This page intentionally does not repeat the historic score, false-positive count, or miss count. The earlier version remains part of the repository's Git history, but historical visibility is not approval to reuse those figures.

## What must be completed before republication

A future public benchmark report must include, at minimum:

1. A frozen benchmark version and exact ground-truth inventory.
2. A row-level scoring export that reconciles to every published denominator and numerator.
3. Explicit inclusion, exclusion, taxonomy-credit, and adjudication rules.
4. Run conditions, environment details, and reproducibility records.
5. A clear separation between raw results and any adjusted or adjudicated view.
6. A description of benchmark ownership and the absence or presence of independent evaluation.
7. A limitations section that prevents internal results from being generalized to arbitrary real-world applications.
8. A reviewed publication statement approved against the source evidence.

## Current public guidance

Do not use historic G1 figures in the README, website, sales material, investor material, or partner outreach as current performance claims. Do not describe the benchmark as independently audited or certified.

The public product position is documented in [Claims and Messaging](CLAIMS.md). The broader standard for responsible validation is in [Evidence and Validation](docs/EVIDENCE_AND_VALIDATION.md).

## Status ownership

The benchmark should be republished only after the source-of-truth row-level evidence, scoring code output, and written explanation reconcile exactly. A corrected narrative without a reconciled evidence export is not sufficient.
