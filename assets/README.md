### Regenerating

```text
python3 scripts/dev/build-brand.py     # brand/*.svg from vulngpt logo.png (needs numpy, Pillow)
node scripts/dev/render-brand.mjs      # brand PNG icons + favicon.ico from the SVGs
python3 scripts/dev/build-images.py    # img/*.webp and manifest.json (Pillow with WebP)
python3 scripts/dev/build-mascot.py    # img/mascot-wave.{png,webp}
node scripts/dev/build-og.mjs          # social/*.png and social-preview.png
```

The Node scripts read `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` (path to an existing `node_modules/playwright`). Run `build-images.py` after `build-mascot.py` so the manifest lists the cutout.

## Publication and brand integrity

Use genuine, current, approved screenshots. Do not alter screenshot content to imply a different product state, fabricate results, or present sample workspace data as customer outcomes. Captures are illustrative records of the interface at a point in time, not proof of current service availability.

The mascot should retain its approved compact rounded-square bug silhouette, two segmented antennae, black square eyes with purple pixel pupils, short arms, and small feet. Do not introduce extra limbs, clothing, wings, a permanent mouth, or a redesigned head/body split.

Keep screenshots free of confidential customer information.
