Five real screens, one investigation.

Each screen below is an unedited capture from the VulnGPT private beta. For every one: what you are looking at, what it demonstrates, and what it does not show.

Capture context. These are static captures, not a live session. Counts, names and statuses show the captured state only. They are not live telemetry, customer outcomes or a guarantee of current availability. The Elite Red Team Reports capture shows report generation and download disabled by export policy.

01 of 05

Dashboard

What you are looking at

The overview of a completed scan named Payments API. A findings summary sits above a severity distribution with bars for critical, high, medium, low and info, and the first rows of the findings list follow. The sidebar groups Targets, Scans, Findings, Attack paths and Access & identities under Testing.

What it demonstrates

That scan status, severity spread and the review queue can be read together. A critical finding on invoice lookup is already marked confirmed, and one finding is waiting for a verdict.

Limits of this capture

The sidebar shows the engine as offline in this capture. Counts, names and times are the captured state only.

02 of 05

Findings queue

What you are looking at

The Findings page with the confirmed filter selected: seven findings, newest first. Each row has a severity, a title, a category such as Authorization or Business logic, a status and an age.

What it demonstrates

Triage by verdict (all, confirmed, provisional, rejected) and by severity. The categories span authorization, business logic and information disclosure.

Limits of this capture

Only the confirmed filter is shown. The provisional and rejected views are not part of this capture.

03 of 05

A BOLA finding in detail

What you are looking at

A critical, confirmed finding titled Broken object-level authorization in invoice lookup. The description says a member session can reach an invoice that belongs to another account by changing the resource identifier. Panels cover status history, operator feedback, a targeted retest and an Evidence section.

What it demonstrates

A finding that names the rule that broke (ownership must be checked server-side on every object read), and a retest that replays only the finding's confirmed path instead of relaunching a full scan.

Limits of this capture

The capture ends at the top of the Evidence section, so the evidence body is not visible. Status history is empty and the Explainability panel reports no active data. The retest control appears with no retest result.

04 of 05

Live Vision

What you are looking at

A recorded-scan view labelled Scanner perspective. Summary tiles show items mapped, routes discovered, recent activity, potential issues and data sources. Below them sit an application activity map with Surface, Workflow, Identity, Testing and Evidence layers, a details panel and an investigation timeline.

What it demonstrates

That a run's activity is recorded and can be inspected. The map links a target, account-ownership boundaries, actors, invoice records and operations, and the timeline lists events such as finding verified.

Limits of this capture

This is a recorded scan, not a live session. No item is selected, and the map is small at this size, so open the full-size image to read it.

05 of 05

Elite Red Team Reports

What you are looking at

The Elite Red Team Reports page with Reports, Narrative, Predictive and Cross-domain tabs, and a list of two reports with their ages.

What it demonstrates

That reports are organized per scan, and that export is controlled by policy. A banner states that report generation and download are disabled by effective export policy while existing report metadata stays readable.

Limits of this capture

Generation and download are disabled by export policy in this capture, so no report content is shown. It is not evidence of what a report contains.
